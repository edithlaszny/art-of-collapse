# Art of Collapse — An Overlap Diagram

An interactive diagram and a notation proposal.

Six collapses are usually drawn as a queue. They are not a queue. Their events are
ordered; their processes run concurrently; and one of the six is not a member of the
series at all — it is the ground the other five stand on.

The page sets out the notation ("overlap diagram": frame, lane, extent, heat, trace,
tick, substrate, ghost), states what the notation deliberately cannot encode, and
applies it to a worked instance. The horizontal axis is in dimensionless phase units.
Nothing here is a forecast, and the model behind the curves is a toy: six logistic
decays with rates chosen for legibility.
